# Product parameter design

## Stage gates

| Stage | Minimum evidence | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | target customer, need, jurisdiction, repayment source | hypothesis and parameter ranges |
| Feasibility | competitor/regulatory constraints, preliminary risk and cost inputs | recommended structure and base economics |
| Approval | sourced assumptions, policy rules, model validation, scenarios | approval package and open conditions |
| Pilot | system/process readiness, limits, monitoring thresholds | pilot terms, caps, stop rules |
| Repricing | actual cohort, funnel, risk, prepayment, recovery and cost data | calibrated parameters and change impact |

## Parameter checklist

Define country/province/city and entity/product scope; customer and use case; eligibility and exclusions; credit purpose; amount minimum/maximum and rounding; term and availability period; interest basis; repayment method including equal principal and interest; fee type, timing, payer, recipient, purchase rate and refund rule; repayment and due-date rules; grace period; prepayment; 0+/30+/90+ Vintage; collateral/guarantee; renewal and repeat borrowing; channel; documentation; disbursement restrictions; product caps; suspension and exit.

For every parameter record:

`parameter | proposed value/range | rationale | source | constraint | customer impact | risk impact | profit impact | system impact | owner | approval status`

## Consistency checks

- Customer repayment frequency must match income/cash-flow frequency.
- Pricing must cover funding, expected loss, acquisition, operations, collection, tax, and capital hurdle under the approved scenario.
- Amount and term must be compatible with affordability, collateral life, data horizon, and funding tenor.
- Eligibility and risk rules must use legally obtainable, operationally available fields.
- Product text, model schedule, system configuration, contract, disclosure, and accounting treatment must use the same rate and fee definitions.
- Optional bundled services must be separated from mandatory charges; use purchase rate for expected economics and the applicable regional rule for APR inclusion.
- Discount rate, pricing cap, VAT base/rate, and disclosure method must have current official sources matching the region and entity/product scope.
- Pilot limits and stop rules must be measurable from available data.
